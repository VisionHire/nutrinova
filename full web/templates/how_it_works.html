How NutriNova Works

From personalized nutrition to smart tracking—manage your health in simple, powerful steps.

STEP 01

Personalized Dashboard

Start by creating your profile with basic details like age, weight, height, and goals. NutriNova analyzes your data and generates a personalized dashboard with daily targets for calories, protein, carbs, fats, and water—designed specifically for your body.

STEP 02

Meal Planner

Plan your daily meals in advance and view them directly on your dashboard. Each meal includes an Eat or Deny option. Only meals marked as "Eat" are added to your nutrition data, ensuring accurate tracking across your dashboard, meal tracker, and calorie tracker.

STEP 03

Meal Entry

Log the food you actually eat by entering food name and quantity. Your entries are securely stored for 30 days and synced with your nutrition system to generate accurate reports and track your daily intake.

STEP 04

Nutrition Report

Generate and download detailed nutrition reports instantly based on your meal entries. Select a custom date range to analyze your eating patterns and track your progress over time.

STEP 05

Nutrition Analyzer

Explore detailed nutritional values of foods from our database. View macronutrients, micronutrients, and trace minerals per 100g to make smarter food choices or use it for research and comparison.

STEP 06

Calorie Tracker

Set your daily calorie goal and monitor your intake in real time. Calories are updated only when meals are marked as "Eat" or logged manually. Visual charts and a 7-day progress view help you stay consistent and achieve your fitness goals.
